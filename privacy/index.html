1. Who we are

This website is operated by OmniTax Professionals Pty Ltd (we, us, or our), a tax advisory practice based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. We are a registered tax agent with the Tax Practitioners Board (TPB) and a registered practice entity member of Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CAANZ).

Our website is available at www.omnitax.com.au.

2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information as defined in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (Privacy Act), including the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

This policy applies when you:

  • browse or interact with our public website;
  • submit an enquiry through our contact form;
  • apply for a role through our careers page;
  • read materials on our Insights page; and
  • communicate with us by email or other channels linked from this website

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies separately.

3. Personal information we collect

The types of personal information we may collect include:

3.1 Information you provide directly

  • Contact and enquiry forms: name, email address, phone number, organisation, area of interest, and message content;
  • Careers applications: name, email, phone, message, and résumé/CV and cover letter files you upload;
  • General correspondence: any personal information you include in emails or other communications with us.

3.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, our hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically collect technical information such as:

  • IP address, browser type and version, device type, and operating system;
  • pages visited, date and time of access, referring URL, and general geographic location (derived from IP address); and
  • server and security logs used to maintain, protect, and improve the website.

3.3 Sensitive information

We do not actively seek sensitive information (such as health information or tax file numbers) through our public website forms. Please do not submit sensitive information via website forms unless we have expressly requested it. If you include sensitive information in a free-text field, we will handle it only to the extent necessary to respond to your enquiry and in accordance with applicable law and professional obligations.

4. How we collect personal information

We collect personal information:

  • directly from you when you complete a form, apply for a role, email us, or use portal features;
  • automatically through cookies, server logs, and similar technologies (see section 8); and
  • from third-party service providers that process submissions on our behalf (for example, form delivery services).

Where reasonable, we collect personal information only from you. If we receive information about you from a third party, we will take reasonable steps to ensure you are aware of the collection and the matters set out in this policy.

5. Why we use personal information

We collect, hold, use, and disclose personal information for purposes including:

  • responding to enquiries and communicating with prospective and existing clients;
  • assessing and managing job applications;
  • providing, maintaining, and improving our website and services;
  • operating a secure client portal and document access when available;
  • publishing Insights and firm information you choose to access;
  • protecting our website, systems, and users from fraud, abuse, and security threats;
  • complying with legal, regulatory, and professional obligations (including TPB and CAANZ requirements); and
  • establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims where necessary.

If we wish to use your personal information for a purpose that is not reasonably expected from the circumstances of collection, we will obtain your consent where required by law.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Form processing providers: website forms may be transmitted via third-party form services (such as FormSubmit) that forward submissions to our designated email addresses;
  • Technology and hosting providers: entities that host our website, provide email, authentication, database, or document management services;
  • Font and content delivery services: for example, Google Fonts, which may receive technical data when fonts are loaded by your browser;
  • Anti-abuse and security services: where used on forms or login pages to prevent spam and unauthorised access;
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, auditors, or insurers where reasonably necessary;
  • Regulators and authorities: where required or authorised by law; and
  • Successors: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information. We require service providers that handle personal information on our behalf to use it only for the purposes we specify and to apply appropriate security measures, where practicable under contractual arrangements.

7. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers may store or process data outside Australia (for example, in the United States or the European Union). Where personal information is disclosed overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure the overseas recipient complies with the APPs or is subject to a substantially similar privacy regime, unless an APP exception applies.

By submitting information through our website, you acknowledge that your information may be processed by overseas service providers for the purposes described in this policy.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to enable core functionality, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used.

8.1 Types of cookies

  • Strictly necessary: required for basic site operation and security;
  • Functional: remember choices you make (where implemented); and
  • Analytics: help us understand traffic and usage patterns if analytics tools are enabled.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. Third-party services linked from our site (such as Google policies referenced on form pages) may set their own cookies under their terms.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. Measures may include access controls, secure transmission (HTTPS), password policies for portal accounts, and limiting access to staff and contractors who need the information for their role.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of an eligible data breach under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, we will assess the incident and, where required, notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

10. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy legal, accounting, regulatory, and professional record-keeping requirements.

Enquiry and application records are generally retained for a period we consider reasonable for business administration and compliance, after which they are securely deleted or de-identified where no longer required.

11. Access, correction, and complaints

Under the Privacy Act, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you and request correction if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 15. We may need to verify your identity before providing access. We will respond within a reasonable period and in accordance with the Privacy Act. In some circumstances we may refuse access where permitted by law; if so, we will explain our reasons.

11.1 Complaints

If you believe we have breached the APPs or mishandled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the OAIC at www.oaic.gov.au.

12. Direct marketing

We may use your contact details to send information about our services where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe facility in a message or by contacting us. Opting out of marketing does not affect our ability to send transactional or service-related messages.

13. Children

Our website is directed at adults and business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without parental or guardian consent. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated by other reasonable means where appropriate. Your continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.
