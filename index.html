About OmniTax Professionals

A firm dedicated to providing quality tax services.

OmniTax Professionals is comprised of tax practitioners with significant experience in "Big 4" accounting firms who apply a fresh approach to meeting our clients' needs.

We provide a value add, practical and solutions-driven service and have experience and knowledge across various tax requirements including providing support for mergers, acquisitions, divestments, refinancings, capital restructures, public offerings, fund and platform establishments, regulator submissions and liaison, tax consolidation, tax modelling, tax distribution/provision calculations, unit pricing, compliance lodgements, tax governance and risk management.

OmniTax Professionals is a registered tax agent with the Tax Practitioners Board (TPB) and a registered practice entity member of Chartered Accountants Australia & New Zealand (CAANZ).

Though we are based in Brisbane (Australia), we service clients throughout Australia and globally.
